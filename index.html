What MovieHub does

MovieHub searches the OMDb database by title and shows the matches as a poster grid. Open any poster to get its full details page.

  • Fast Search

    Type a title and the matching films appear in seconds.

  • Clean Interface

    Posters and titles come first, with the details one click away.

  • Always Ready

    No account and nothing to install. Open the page and search.
